The review queue, by day

Review is the week's new transactions read like a bank statement, grouped by day, with D's other kinds pinned above the days and D's Home card, one chip per kind and its count. Everything is invented and clickable. Tap a tick to approve, tap a category to change it, tap a row for what was bought and the split, and on a phone swipe a row right to approve or left for a category. Thumb reach shows where a one-handed thumb lands, and Theme switches between light, dark and the system setting.
